- **Category**: SaaS & Productivity (Rank #6)
- **Primary Design Style**: Expressive Pastel Gradients / Glassmorphic

- **Conversion Goal**: Drive user acquisition and engagement through Expressive Pastel Gradients / Glassmorphic aesthetics and clear value proposition.

### B. Scope Plane
- **Core Interactive Features**: Vertical tab bar navigation; collapsible command launcher; space switcher pills; playful onboarding flow.

### C. Structure Plane (IA & IxD)

### D. Skeleton Plane (Layout & Grids)
- **Container Geometry**: Centered responsive container with standard desktop max-width constraints.
- **Layout Rhythm**: Structured grid systems separating high-impact hero zones from modular feature components.

### E. Surface Plane (Sensory & Visuals)
